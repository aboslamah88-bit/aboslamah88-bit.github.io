The Studio Way

How we make games

Principles behind every game that carries the Abu Salama signature.

Authentic identity

Details from our land and culture, original artwork made for each game, and an Arabic-first interface with full English support.

Privacy first

No accounts, no sign-up. Your progress stays on your device, and we run no servers collecting your play data.

Play without interruptions

No ads start automatically while you solve a puzzle. Rewarded videos are optional. Depending on the game, ads may appear between orders, with a one-time purchase to remove them.
