Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Ilom Al Majlis and its support pages handle data. The developer is Abdulrhman Alatawi, operating as Abu Salama Games, in Saudi Arabia. Contact: aboslamah88@icloud.com.

Local progressAdvertisingYour choicesApple servicesRetention and rights

1. Progress and settings on your device

The game stores progress, night ratings, daily challenge results and preferences, such as language and sound, locally on your device. You do not need a developer account to play. We do not operate a service to sync progress between devices. Apple-managed device backups may include this data, depending on your settings.

2. Rewarded advertising and advertising services

The game uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game help. It also bundles an AppLovin mediation adapter. Its presence alone does not mean AppLovin demand is enabled or serves ads to every player. Each provider’s involvement depends on advertising settings, consent and service availability.

When advertising services run, they and their partners may process IP addresses for approximate location, device or app identifiers and the advertising identifier where permitted, ad viewing and interaction data, product interactions, crash reports and performance data. This supports ad delivery, measurement, service improvement and fraud prevention. The current game requests non-personalized rewarded ads. These ads may still use identifiers for frequency capping and reporting, with data handling subject to your permissions, consent and applicable law. The game does not request precise GPS location permission.

See the Google Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and AppLovin Privacy Policy for their practices.

3. Tracking permission and consent

The game requests Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before tracking that requires it. You can decline and continue playing. Declining prevents use of the IDFA advertising identifier for ads; services may still process other data needed to deliver and measure permitted ads, subject to available choices and applicable law.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent messages where applicable. An ad privacy option appears in game settings when required by the platform so you can change your choices. You can also manage tracking permission under the device’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and review Google ad choices.

4. Game Center and purchases

When you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game sends reputation scores and achievements to Apple for leaderboards and achievements. Your nickname, avatar and scores may be visible to other players; activity visibility follows your Game Center settings. Manage sign-in and sharing in your device settings. See Game Center & Privacy.

Apple processes the No Ads purchase and its restoration through StoreKit. The No Ads feature stops rewarded-ad viewing; purchasing it does not itself disable all data processing by service SDKs. The game checks purchase status and transaction information to enable the feature; we do not receive your payment card number or bank details. Apple retains purchase records under its policies. See App Store & Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Support messages and website visits

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may process request data, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Sharing and security

Data for the services above is sent to their providers when those services are used and may be processed outside your country under their policies. We do not sell support messages. We may retain or disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and prevent abuse. We use reasonable safeguards for information we receive, although no transmission or storage is entirely free of risk.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains on the device until app data is removed; a copy may remain in Apple-managed backups. Deleting the game does not automatically delete Apple purchase records, Game Center activity or data already processed by advertising services. Those services set their own retention periods.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to address the request and meet legal obligations. You can email us to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information you provided, subject to the rights available in your country. For Apple or advertising-provider data, also use the privacy tools and contact methods in their policies.

8. Children and families

Parents and guardians can manage purchases, tracking and Game Center features through Apple’s controls and Screen Time restrictions. If you believe a child sent us personal information in a support message without the required permission, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate action.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when game services or data practices change. The latest update date appears above, and material changes will be communicated appropriately. For privacy questions or requests, email aboslamah88@icloud.com.
