Your privacy in Falcon Workshop.

What stays on your device, and how ads, purchases and support work.

Developer and contact

Developer: Abdulrahman Alatawi (Abu Salama Games), Saudi Arabia. For privacy questions or to delete support emails, write to aboslamah88@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

The game stores on your device your order progress, seals, mithqal balance, purchased decorations, gift calendar, settings (sound, language, music), your age-question answer and rating-prompt timing. We run no game accounts or sync server and upload none of this to a server of ours. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads and privacy

The game uses Google AdMob. No ads load before the age question is answered, and they stay off for players under the required age. The game shows Google’s consent form where it applies and may ask Apple’s “Allow tracking” permission; if you decline, ads continue without that tracking. We request ads rated for general audiences (PG).

Google’s SDK may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), ads shown and interactions with them, and crash and performance data, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reopen “Ad privacy choices” in the game’s settings when available. See Google’s policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

The game does not ask for precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for the “Gold Bundle”, “Unlimited hints” and “Remove ads”, all one-time purchases with no subscription. We never receive your card number. The app checks entitlements through StoreKit and runs no server for transactions. Apple keeps purchase records under its privacy policy.

The developer website

The site adds no trackers or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process request data to serve and secure it under its privacy statement.

Children

The game suits all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. No ads are shown to players who indicate they are under the required age.

Support

When you email us we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them only to reply and fix the problem, keeping them as long as needed. You can ask us to delete your emails.

Changes

We update this page when the game or its services change, and change the date above.
