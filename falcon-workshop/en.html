Every jewel has a story

Welcome to the falcon’s shop.

A jewellery-crafting puzzle: 200 orders, customers with stories and a shop you dress yourself. In Arabic and English on iPhone and iPad.

Coming soon to the App Store

How do I play?

A customer comes to the falcon’s shop with a jewellery order. Drag gem pieces from the tray onto the molds on the bench; each slot only takes the gem drawn in it. Pieces do not rotate, and a filled mold is forged into the jewel. Every order has a goal: forge all its molds. It may also require a waste, placement or cut limit, a delivery order, or keeping specified pieces in the tray.

What are the cutter, waste and the Mastery Seal?

The cutter splits a piece in two, a limited number of times per order. A stone placed outside the molds counts as waste. You earn the Mastery Seal by forging an order with the least waste and the fewest cuts that order allows.

Can I lose?

No. Undo is always free and you can restart at any time. At a dead end the master may offer an optional “last chance”; an order finished with it counts, but the Mastery Seal stays for an unaided attempt.

What are mithqals?

The shop’s currency, earned by playing: for each order’s first forge, its first Mastery Seal, and a seven-day gift calendar that builds up to a big gift on day seven. Missed days never cost you anything. Spend them on decorations for the bench, the shop and your house.

How do hints work?

The first hint in an order is free. After that you can choose to watch a video for a hint, or buy “Unlimited hints” to make every hint free without videos.

Are there ads?

No ads start before a short age question shown after the first orders; if you are under the required age they stay off. Rewarded videos only appear when you choose them: for a hint, a last chance, or to double today’s gift. An ad may sometimes appear between orders after you finish one, never during play. “Remove ads” or the “Gold Bundle” stops ads between orders.

What can I buy?

All one-time purchases, no subscription: the “Gold Bundle” (500 mithqals, no ads between orders and unlimited hints), “Unlimited hints” and “Remove ads”. The App Store shows the price in your account’s currency before you confirm.

I bought something and it did not appear

In the shop’s storeroom choose “Restore purchases” with the Apple Account that bought it, while online. A pending purchase unlocks only once Apple approves it. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device: orders, seals, mithqals, decorations and settings. There is no game account or cross-device sync, and deleting the app may delete progress. You can play offline; ads, purchases and restore need a connection.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Email aboslamah88@icloud.com. Mention the order number, game version and device, and attach a screenshot if you can. Never send passwords or card details.
