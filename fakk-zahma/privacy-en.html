Your privacy in Unjam the Traffic.

This policy explains what stays on your device and how advertising, purchases and support work.

Developer and contact

The developer is Abdulrhman Alatawi, Saudi Arabia. For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, email aboslamah88@icloud.com.

Game progress

The game stores level progress, moves, results, hint credits, sound and language preferences, and review-prompt timing on your device. We do not operate game accounts or a progress-sync server, and do not upload these records to a developer-operated server. There is no cross-device progress sync.

Delete the app to remove its local data. Device backups may affect restoration. Restarting a level does not delete all records, and restoring purchases does not restore level progress.

Support pages

Support pages do not add tracking tools, cookies or a data-collection form. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process access requests, including IP address, time and browser information, to deliver and secure the service under its privacy statement.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices. Advertising services may initialize when the game opens. Ad requests are gated by the consent system's eligibility result; an ad is shown after you choose to watch it. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ads viewed and interactions, crash information, and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate and improve its services. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing and may use identifiers. Actual processing depends on the device, service settings and privacy choices.

The game does not request precise location, contacts, microphone or camera access, or ask for permission to track activity across apps. You can revisit Ad privacy options inside the game when available. Google's retention practices and data controls are described in its privacy policy. See also Google's mobile ads data disclosure.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for Unlimited hints, a one-time purchase with no subscription. We do not receive your payment-card number or security code. The app verifies purchase entitlements locally through StoreKit to unlock and restore the feature. We do not operate a server storing purchase transactions or payment details. Apple retains purchase records under its privacy policy; deleting the app does not delete those records.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. AdMob requests are non-personalized with a general-audience content rating.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply and resolve the issue, and retain them as needed to handle your request. You may request deletion through the support email; information needed for a legal obligation may be retained. Please avoid unnecessary personal information.

Policy updates

We update this page and its date when the game or its services change.
