How do I play?

Slide vehicles along their direction to clear a path for the marked Cressida to the exit on the right. Vehicles cannot rotate. Each continuous drag counts as one move, even across several spaces.

How do I get the iPhone or iPad app?

Download Unjam the Traffic for free from the App Store. It requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later, and updates arrive through the App Store.

How do stars work?

Match the optimal move count for three stars. Up to three extra moves earn two stars; more earn one. Replay any level to improve your result.

Can I play offline?

The app includes every puzzle and vehicle, so you can play offline. Ads, purchases, purchase restoration and this page need an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress stays on your device. There is no game account or cross-device sync. On returning, you can continue or start a new game. Deleting the app may remove its progress; restarting a level keeps earlier results.

How do hints and rewarded ads work?

The first two hints in each level are free. You can then choose a rewarded ad to receive three saved hint credits when the provider confirms the reward. An extra hint uses one credit, and no credit is spent unless a valid hint is produced. Closing an ad before earning its reward does not add credits.

If an ad is unavailable, a complimentary hint is offered and you can keep playing.

Will ads interrupt play?

There are no banner ads or ads between levels. A rewarded ad appears only when you choose it for hints, and you can keep solving without watching one.

What does Unlimited hints include?

One purchase unlocks hints across all levels without watching ads. There is no subscription. Apple's localized price appears before confirmation. If the store says the purchase is unavailable, you can keep playing while it becomes available.

I purchased hints but they are still locked

Open the Shop and select Restore purchases while connected to the internet, using the Apple Account that made the purchase. A pending purchase unlocks only after Apple's approval. Restoration unlocks hints; it does not transfer progress from another device.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How can I report a problem?

Email aboslamah88@icloud.com with the mode, level, game version and device model, plus a screenshot if useful. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.
